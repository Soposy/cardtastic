Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Cardtastic, a vocabulary learning app developed by Maximilian Pilartz. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

Privacy-first by design: Cardtastic stores all your data locally on your device. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no tracking. The vast majority of your data never leaves your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

All of the following data is created by you and stored exclusively on your device in a local database:

  • Vocabulary cards (words, translations, example sentences)
  • Flashcard decks and their language settings
  • Learning progress and statistics (phases, streaks, review dates)
  • Custom chat scenarios and AI conversation history
  • App preferences (language, theme, notification settings)
We have no access to this data. It is never transmitted to our servers.

Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable cloud backup, your local database file can be synced to a third-party provider of your choice:

  • Google Drive – Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft OneDrive – Microsoft Privacy Policy
  • Apple iCloud – Apple Privacy Policy
  • WebDAV – your own server

We do not act as an intermediary and have no access to your synced data. Authentication tokens for cloud services are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never shared with us.

Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions. All are optional.

Permission Purpose Optional?
Microphone Voice input for AI conversation mode Yes
Speech Recognition Transcribing spoken answers in chat Yes
Camera Scanning text from books via OCR Yes
Photo Library Importing images for text recognition Yes
Notifications Learning reminders and streak alerts Yes

You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings. Denying a permission only disables that specific feature.

On-Device AI

Cardtastic offers an optional AI feature powered by an open-source language model (Gemma 2B) that runs entirely on your device.

  • No data is sent to external AI services or the cloud.
  • All translations, suggestions, and AI conversations are processed locally.
  • The AI model (~1.2 GB) is downloaded directly to your device and stored locally.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs (IDFA)
  • Usage analytics, session data, or crash reports
  • Location data
  • Any data through third-party analytics or tracking SDKs

Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Removing individual cards or decks within the app
  • Using "Delete All Data" in the app's settings
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all local data immediately)

If you used cloud sync, please also delete the backup file directly from your cloud storage provider.

Children's Privacy

Cardtastic does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or submission of any personal data. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns, please contact us.
